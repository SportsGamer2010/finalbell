# Final Bell — Controls (Undisputed Competitor scheme)

Face buttons and the number row throw punches. The right stick angles the fighter toward the opponent and cannot turn them fully away. A hard right-stick flick still throws one punch. The punch name appears on screen when it is thrown. Camera orbit is mouse / Q-E.

| Action | PlayStation | Xbox | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move / dodge | L stick | L stick | WASD |
| Jab | Square | X | 1, J, or left mouse |
| Straight (cross) | Triangle | Y | 2, K, or right mouse |
| Lead hook | X | A | 3 or U |
| Rear hook | Circle | B | 4 or I |
| Lead uppercut | RS down-left, or L1+lead hook | RS down-left, or LB+lead hook | 5 or Y |
| Rear uppercut | RS down-right, or L1+rear hook | RS down-right, or LB+rear hook | 6 or H |
| Body version of any punch | Hold L1, then the punch | Hold LB, then the punch | Hold C, then the punch |
| Power version | Hold R1, then the punch | Hold RB, then the punch | Hold V, then the punch |
| Angle (right stick) | RS left/right, still facing them | same | — |
| RS punch flick | Up-left jab, up-right straight, left hook, right hook, down uppercut | same | — |
| Block (hold) | R2 | RT | Left Shift |
| Weave (hold) | L2 | LT | Left Ctrl |
| Camera orbit | — | — | Mouse / Q-E |

A solo match is you plus one opponent. The ring map also contains a placed boxer; that extra body is removed when the match starts. Two human players do not get an AI opponent.

Mode select: on-screen menu, or console `FBMode 1` (AI) / `2` (MP) / `3` (Training).

What each gameplay function does is listed in `docs/CodeGuide.md`.
